# 📊 Sales Snapshot Performance Dashboard 

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Sales Performance Dashboard built using Power BI to analyze sales, revenue, profit, customer behavior, and territory performance. It contains four report pages that provide actionable business insights through KPIs, charts, tables, and interactive filters.

🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze total revenue, profit, orders, and quantity sold.
- Track monthly sales and revenue trends.
- Understand customer demographics and purchasing behavior.
- Compare sales performance across countries, regions, and territories.
- Identify product returns and high-performing product categories.

🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Power BI – Dashboard development and visualization
- Power Query – Data cleaning and transformation
- DAX – Measures and KPI calculations
- Data Visualization – Charts, tables, slicers, and KPI cards

📈 Dashboard Pages

1. Sales Snapshot – Performance Report

Provides an overview of monthly revenue, order quantity, education-level analysis, customer distribution, and key business KPIs.

2. Sales Overview

Analyzes high-value orders, homeownership, product returns, monthly revenue, total cost, and product performance.

3. Territory Performance

Compares revenue and profit across regions and countries using geographical maps, tables, bar charts, and KPI cards.

4. Customer Insights

Explores customer demographics, education levels, occupations, marital status, gender, average retail price, and customer revenue.

💡 Key Insights

- Total Revenue: Approximately 24.91 million.
- Total Profit: Approximately 10.46 million.
- Total Orders: 56,046.
- Total Customers: 18,148.
- Quantity Sold: 84,174.
- Monthly revenue peaks around June and drops sharply in July.
- Australia and the United States are among the leading revenue-generating countries.
- Customer education and occupation categories show differences in customer counts and order quantities.
